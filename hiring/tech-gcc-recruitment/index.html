The problem we solve

Most tech hiring in India runs on keyword matching — a title on a JD gets matched to a title on a resume, and the search is considered done. That breaks down fastest at senior and leadership roles, where two identical-looking resumes can represent completely different levels of readiness for your specific team's stage. We built our screening process around catching that gap before it becomes your problem.

What we hire for

  • Engineering leadership (Engineering Managers, Directors, VP Engineering) for GCCs and product companies
  • GCC leadership — Country Heads, Centre Heads, and functional leaders across build- and scale-stage centres
  • Data & analytics roles (Data Engineers, Analytics Engineers, Data Scientists, ML Engineers)
  • Product roles (PMs, POs, Product Leaders)
  • Core software engineering, mid-level ICs through Staff/Principal
  • IT services and product engineering delivery leadership

How we screen

We evaluate leadership candidates for stage-fit as much as capability — an early-stage GCC still defining its operating model needs a different leader than a mature GCC scaling delivery, even when both resumes look senior. For technical and data roles, we look past adjacent-sounding titles (a Data Engineer and an Analytics Engineer are not interchangeable) to what the role actually requires day to day.
