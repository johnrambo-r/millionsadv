The problem we solve

Search "RTL Design" or "Physical Design" on any resume database and you'll get results that look right and hire wrong. The title has been commoditised — a candidate who runs EDA tools competently and a candidate who understands the engineering reasoning behind the flow can carry an identical designation. We've built our technical screening specifically to catch that gap before it costs you a mis-hire.

What we hire for

  • RTL Design and Verification Engineers
  • Physical Design Engineers
  • Analog/Mixed-Signal Design Engineers
  • DFT (Design for Test) Engineers
  • Semiconductor GCC leadership and engineering management
  • Embedded systems and firmware engineers for chip and hardware teams
  • Silicon validation and post-silicon debug roles

How we screen

Our technical screening goes past tool familiarity — Cadence, Synopsys, and Mentor experience shows up on every resume in this space. We evaluate the reasoning behind a candidate's design decisions, not just their exposure to the tools, because that's what actually predicts whether they can operate independently on your team.
